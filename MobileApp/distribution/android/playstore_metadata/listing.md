```
Indie Playbook is the premier directory and knowledge base of the top independent mobile apps built with modern cross-platform frameworks: React Native, Flutter, and Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP).

Whether you are an indie maker, mobile developer, or entrepreneur, Indie Playbook helps you discover how successful indie apps are engineered, launched, and monetized.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

📱 Curated Indie Directory
Explore top-ranking independent apps across App Store and Google Play categories. View real download estimates, ratings, publishers, and release timelines.

🛠️ Multiplatform Framework Insights
Filter apps by tech stack — Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP), Flutter, and React Native — to discover real-world production architectures and frameworks powering indie success.

📲 Store Presence Tracking
Easily distinguish between Dual Store apps (published on both Google Play and Apple App Store) and App Store exclusives.

🌱 Origin Stories & 📈 Growth Playbooks
Learn what motivated founders to build their first version, how they validated their ideas, and the viral loops, ASO, and marketing strategies they used to reach thousands of users.

📐 Data-Driven Methodology
Built on comprehensive App Store ranking intelligence and download metrics powered by AppFigures.

💡 Community App Submissions
Nominate your own cross-platform indie app or suggest edits to help the playbook grow.

Start exploring the indie mobile ecosystem today with Indie Playbook!
```
